1-Overview of the Ma7ali Application

Your All-in-One Mobile POS Solution – Simple, Smart, and Built for Your Business

This application is a complete Point of Sale (POS) and inventory management system designed specifically for small to medium-sized businesses that want speed, simplicity, and full control—all from a mobile or tablet device.

What You Can Expect:

  • Quick Sales Processing:
    Sell faster and smarter with an easy-to-use cart system, on-screen product lists, and real-time updates.
  • Inventory Control:
    Add, update, and organize your products by category, barcode, or stock level. Instantly see what's in stock, low on stock, or out of stock.
  • Smart Dashboards:
    Get powerful insights with interactive charts showing your daily, weekly, and monthly sales, profits, best-selling products, and more.
  • Customer Management:
    Keep track of your customers and their debt balances, with full history and details per customer.
  • PDF Reports & Export:
    Export sales records, product lists, customer debts, and performance charts into professional PDF files with one tap.
  • Multi-User Ready:
    Each user gets their own data. Whether you manage one store or many, your information stays separate and secure.
  • Offline Support:
    No internet? No problem. Work offline and sync data automatically when you’re back online.
  • Mobile-Optimized & PWA:
    Works like an app directly from your home screen—no download needed. Fast, responsive, and always with you.

Why You’ll Love It:

  • Saves you time by automating sales and inventory tracking.
  • Helps you make better business decisions with real-time data and smart insights.
  • Keeps you organized with simple tools that just work.
  • Gives you full visibility over your store’s performance anytime, from your phone or tablet.


2. Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add resources to the store?

To add resources (Product-Customer-Sales-Carts):

  • press the + icon located in the bottom of the screen to open adder section
  • press the icon that represents the rescource you want to add, and you will be directed to add it to the store

How do I view and edit customer's debt and details?

To view details:

  • Press the second icon located in the bottom of the screen to the right to view the customers in the store
  • Search a customer with phone number or name.
  • Click on the customer you want to view the details
  • You can then edit customer details or remove the customer.

To add debt:

  • From the debt details click the button "Add"
  • Enter the details and balance and press "Add"

How to update product stock and info?

To update a product:

  • Press "Search Product" in the top of the Screen.
  • Type the product Name or Label.
  • Click on the product to open update product tool.
  • From the update product tool update the stock to the value you want.
  • You can also update any product field like cost price, profit and image.

What should I do if I accidently added a product to the cart?

If you accidenly added a product or quantity to the cart you can cancel the cart and start a new one by clicking the "Cancel Cart" button

What should I do if I accidently added a product to the sale?

If you accidenly added a product to the sale you can cancel or remove it from sales from the add to sale section.
To do that:

  • press on + icon in the bottom of the screen then click on the icon that has a + symbol in a square.
  • Search for the product you want to cancel with the label or barcode
  • Click on cancel and the product will be canceled from sales and returned to the stock

Why can't I find a product when I search it?

If you cant find a product when you search it you probably miss typed the product label or barcode.
product Label and Barcode are you unique to the product, so make sure you double check the poduct you are adding and searching.

How do I set the currency to LBP or $ on default?

You can select the default currency from the "Store Setting" button in the side bar. Select the currency you want and press "Save Setting".



3. User Manual

Manage Products

To correctly manage your products follow these instructions:

  • When you add or update a new product enter carefully products info to avoid wrong calculations, analysis and details.
  • You can always swtich the currency from the button located in the top screen and enter the cost and profit of the product based on the currency.
  • You can filter the products in the products view section how ever you desire. The filtering also applies to the exportation of the products.
  • If product stock reached 0 the product will still be in the products list, if you want to remove it from the list search for it and remove it.
    Refer to the FAQs to read the instructions on how to remove a product.
  • Profit should always be less than Cost Price or you will get negative values on Total Profits analytics.
    If you accidenlty set a Profit more than the Cost Price refer to the FAQs to read how to update product Profit and Cost Price.

Manage Customers

To correctly manage your customers follow these instructions:

  • When you add a customer make sure to enter the Name and Phone Number correclty to avoid loosing track of the customer.
  • Customer Name and Phone Number are both unique to facilate identefying customers.
    So when you add or update a customer you will be notified if the Name or Phone Number already exist in your customers list.
  • You only need to add a customer when he has debt. So when the customer debt is 0 you can remove him/her from the list.
  • You can also remove customer debt and balance if he/she payed for it, you can do that by locating the customer debt and remove it.
  • Use the debt exporting button to export the details to inform the customer about his debt before or after he pays them.

Manage Sales

To correctly manage your sales follow these instructions:

  • Your sales are tracked based on 24 hours local time. So every day you add a sale you will track them in carts and sales section.
  • Sales are added from the "add to sales" and "add carts" sections. So when you start a cart you are also adding to the sales.
  • You can cancel a sale from "add to sales" by locating the product and pressing cancel.
  • When you cancel a cart you also cancel the products added to the cart from the sales.
  • View your sales and carts in the "carts and Sales" section located in the bottm of the screen on the left side of the + icon.
  • Press "View Carts" to see the all the carts you created and the products that are added to each cart (Press on the cart details to see the products that are added to it). You can also search for the cart by the name.
    Note: Carts names are not unique so you can create cart with the same name. But if you want a cart to be unique to help searching for a cart check exsiting carts before creating one.
  • You can filter the sales based on daily, weekly, monthly, yearly and specific sales dates.
  • Use the export sales button under each sales date to export the sales details.

View Dashboard

To boost and manage you store use the dashboard section:

  • Dashboard section provides you with powerfull analytics of your store such as your products inventory and sales.
  • Check your dashboard hourly or daily to keep track of your products stock, sales, revenues, profits and more.
  • Each chart and table in the dashboard provides you with powerfull different analytics, use them to create notes in your notes tool in the side bar to keep track of the things you want to add, update or change.
  • You can also remove customer debt and balance if he/she payed for it, you can do that by locating the customer debt and remove it.
  • Use the debt exporting button to export the details to inform the customer about his debt before or after he pays them.
